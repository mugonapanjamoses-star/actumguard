Frequently Asked Questions

Is this legal advice?

No. ActumGuard is a risk-management and workflow software tool. We map your answers to public regulations and provide remediation checklists. You should always confirm your final obligations with qualified legal counsel.

How is the Risk Score calculated?

Your score is based on the severity of the laws triggered by your answers. Low (1-2): Baseline policy needed. Medium (3-5): Gaps in transparency. High (6-9): Immediate compliance risk. Critical (10+): Urgent legal intervention required.

Does this cover South Africa and the US?

Yes. Our engine currently covers South Africa (POPIA), the EU (AI Act & GDPR), the US (Colorado ADMT, NYC LL144, Illinois HB 3773), and 8 other major jurisdictions.

What happens after I buy the Starter Pack?

Immediately after payment, you will receive an email with a secure link to download your AI Inventory Spreadsheet, Acceptable Use Policy template, Vendor Questionnaire, and the 30-Day Implementation Plan.

ActumGuard is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. Assessments are directional risk-management tools, not legal opinions. Confirm obligations with qualified counsel in each jurisdiction.
