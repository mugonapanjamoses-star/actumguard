Why not just ask a chatbot for a policy?

You can, and a chatbot can draft text. Drafting is the easy part. The harder part is what happens around the text.

What ActumGuard adds

  • A scan that points to the rules relevant to your sector and countries.
  • An inventory of every AI tool you use, so nothing is missed.
  • A vendor questionnaire to ask your AI suppliers the questions regulators care about.
  • A plan and a record of who approved what, and when.

What it does not do

  • It does not replace a lawyer.
  • It does not run bias audits. Some laws require an independent auditor.
  • It does not promise any outcome with a client, insurer or regulator.

What the scan looks at

The scan matches your answers to a set of AI and data rules. It currently includes:

  • POPIA (South Africa)
  • EU AI Act
  • UK GDPR
  • EU GDPR
  • NYC Local Law 144
  • Colorado AI Act
  • US Fair Housing Act

Laws and their start dates change often. Each rule in the scan should show when it was last reviewed. Confirm anything that matters with a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction.

Who this is for. Small businesses that use AI for hiring, customer decisions, scheduling or chat and need to show they have thought about the risks. If you handle regulated health or financial data at scale, use this as a starting point and involve counsel early.
